================================================================================
  MINI12864 CONNECTIONS
================================================================================

  EXP1 (10-pin IDC):
    pin 2  BTN_ENC   ── GPIO 32   encoder push
    pin 3  LCD_RST   ── GPIO 33   display reset
    pin 4  LCD_A0    ── GPIO 25   data/command
    pin 5  —         ── (NC)
    pin 6  —         ── (NC)
    pin 7  NEOPIXEL  ── GPIO 26   RGB backlight
    pin 8  GND       ── GND
    pin 10 5V        ── VIN (5V)

  EXP2 (10-pin IDC):
    pin 2  SCK        ── GPIO 18  SPI clock
    pin 3  ENC_CLK   ── GPIO 34  encoder A  (+ 10kΩ to 3.3V)
    pin 5  ENC_DT    ── GPIO 35  encoder B  (+ 10kΩ to 3.3V)
    pin 6  MOSI      ── GPIO 23  SPI data
    pin 7  LCD_CS    ── GPIO 5   chip select
    pin 8  GND       ── GND

================================================================================
  PULLUP RESISTORS (required for input-only pins)
================================================================================

   GPIO 34 (ENC_CLK) ── 10kΩ ── 3.3V
   GPIO 35 (ENC_DT)  ── 10kΩ ── 3.3V
   GPIO 36 (END_LEFT) ── 10kΩ ── 3.3V
   GPIO 39 (END_RIGHT)── 10kΩ ── 3.3V

================================================================================
  TMC2209 STEPPER DRIVER
================================================================================

                       TMC2209
                ┌───────────────┐
   GPIO 16   ──┤ STEP    A1/M1 ├──┐
   GPIO 17   ──┤ DIR     A2/M2 ├──┤  NEMA 17
   GND (tie) ──┤ EN      B1/M3 ├──┤
   3.3V      ──┤ VIO     B2/M4 ├──┘
   12-24V    ──┤ VM            │
   GND       ──┤ GND           │
                └───────────────┘

  EN is wired to GND permanently — motor always energized.

================================================================================
  ENDSTOPS  (NC — Normally Closed, separate pins)
================================================================================

   Left  endstop ── one leg ── GPIO 36  (+ 10kΩ to 3.3V)
                 └─ other leg ─ GND

   Right endstop ── one leg ── GPIO 39  (+ 10kΩ to 3.3V)
                 └─ other leg ─ GND

================================================================================
  EXTERNAL BUTTONS  (active LOW, INPUT_PULLUP)
================================================================================

   LEFT  button ── one leg ── GPIO 27 ── other leg ── GND
   RIGHT button ── one leg ── GPIO 14 ── other leg ── GND
   PLAY/PAUSE   ── one leg ── GPIO 12 ── other leg ── GND

   SHORT press = tap action   |   HOLD (>400ms) = held action

================================================================================
  HANDHELD CABLE  (controller board ↔ handheld enclosure)
================================================================================

  Cable spec : 16-conductor, 28AWG, flat ribbon, 2.54mm pitch
  Length     : 60cm
  Connectors : 2×8 IDC (16-pin) at both ends
                 Handheld end  — IDC female (crimped onto ribbon)
                 PCB end       — IDC shrouded male header (soldered)
  Strain relief: IDC clip at each end

  SIGNAL INTEGRITY — fit these at the PCB/controller end of the cable:

    NeoPixel data  → 470Ω resistor in series (on PCB, close to output pin)
                     Damps reflections on the 60cm data run.

    ENC_CLK        → 100nF ceramic capacitor to GND
    ENC_DT         → 100nF ceramic capacitor to GND
                     Prevents phantom encoder steps caused by cable noise.

  WIRE ALLOCATION  (16 wires)

    Wire   Signal          Direction    Notes
    ─────  ──────────────  ───────────  ──────────────────────────────
    1      ENC_CLK         H → PCB      Encoder channel A  (+ 100nF to GND at PCB)
    2      ENC_DT          H → PCB      Encoder channel B  (+ 100nF to GND at PCB)
    3      ENC_SW          H → PCB      Encoder push button
    4      LCD_MOSI        PCB → H      SPI data
    5      LCD_SCK         PCB → H      SPI clock
    6      LCD_CS          PCB → H      SPI chip select
    7      LCD_RST         PCB → H      Display reset
    8      LCD_A0          PCB → H      Display data/command
    9      NEOPIXEL        PCB → H      RGB backlight  (+ 470Ω series at PCB)
    10     BTN_LEFT        H → PCB      Left jog button
    11     BTN_RIGHT       H → PCB      Right jog button
    12     BTN_PLAY/PAUSE  H → PCB      Play/pause button
    13     5V              PCB → H      Logic supply for Mini12864 + encoder
    14     GND             Common       Signal ground
    15     GND             Common       Power return (separate for noise isolation)
    16     (spare)         —            Reserved for future use

  H = Handheld enclosure    PCB = Main controller board


================================================================================
  HANDHELD CABLE — OPTION B: DB15 (VGA CABLE)
================================================================================

  Cable spec : Standard VGA cable (DE-15), pre-made, 0.5m
  Connectors : DB15 panel-mount chassis connectors at both ends
                 Handheld end  — DB15 female chassis mount
                 PCB end       — DB15 male chassis mount
  Locking    : Thumbscrew or hex screw at each connector end
  Source     : Any VGA cable 0.5m — extremely common, low cost

  PIN ALLOCATION  (15 pins — pin 15 doubles as extra GND)

    Pin   Signal          Direction    Notes
    ────  ──────────────  ───────────  ──────────────────────────────
    1     ENC_CLK         H → PCB      Encoder A  (+ 100nF to GND at PCB)
    2     ENC_DT          H → PCB      Encoder B  (+ 100nF to GND at PCB)
    3     ENC_SW          H → PCB      Encoder push button
    4     LCD_MOSI        PCB → H      SPI data
    5     LCD_SCK         PCB → H      SPI clock
    6     LCD_CS          PCB → H      SPI chip select
    7     LCD_RST         PCB → H      Display reset
    8     LCD_A0          PCB → H      Display data/command
    9     NEOPIXEL        PCB → H      RGB backlight (+ 470 Ohm series at PCB)
    10    BTN_LEFT        H → PCB      Left jog button
    11    BTN_RIGHT       H → PCB      Right jog button
    12    BTN_PLAY/PAUSE  H → PCB      Play/pause button
    13    5V              PCB → H      Logic supply for Mini12864 + encoder
    14    GND             Common       Signal ground
    15    GND             Common       Power return / extra GND (pin doubled)

  COMPARED TO RIBBON CABLE OPTION:
    Pro  : Pre-made cable, no crimping, locking screws, more robust connector
    Pro  : Panel-mount connectors suit an enclosure design well
    Con  : 15 pins vs 16 — one fewer spare wire
    Con  : VGA cable wiring varies by manufacturer — verify continuity
           before use (some VGA cables omit certain pins)
    Note : Always test with a continuity checker before first use

  H = Handheld enclosure    PCB = Main controller board
